About Me

Designing with intention,
learning every day.

I'm an aspiring UI/UX Designer currently pursuing my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Anurag University (graduating 2026). I create user interfaces in Figma and focus on wireframing, prototyping, and user-centered design.

I'm passionate about designing intuitive digital experiences for web and mobile applications. My work spans academic projects, hackathons, and hands-on UI design — from an AI vehicle health monitoring dashboard to a responsive Apple iPhone landing page redesign.

As a Figma Mentor at Design Forge, I've trained 30+ students in design fundamentals. I published a research paper on Single-Axis Solar Tracking (IJIRT), was a finalist in the National AgriTech Hackathon, and participated in Vision Nova 2k25.

I'm actively seeking opportunities to contribute to innovative products while continuously improving my design and user experience skills.
